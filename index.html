Morning

    Open when the light is interesting

    We started pale hour because the best cafés we knew only got one hour right. Great at dawn, empty by two. Or lovely at night and sad with a cup of coffee at nine.

    So the room changes with the day. The coffee is roasted weekly by a friend down the road, the bread comes out of our own oven before sunrise, and after four the lamps go on and the wine list comes out.

    Good to know

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Always. Tables for parties of five or more are easier with a reservation, especially after four.

    Is there vegetarian and vegan food?

    Most of the menu is vegetarian. Use the filter above to see it. Ask us about vegan swaps, we're happy to adjust.

    Can I bring my laptop?

    Before 11 am, yes. After that we ask you to give the tables back to lunch.

    Do you do takeaway?

    Bread, pastries and coffee, any time we're open. Plates and wine stay in the room.

    Is the space step-free?

    Yes. The entrance on Linden Street has no steps, and there's an accessible restroom.
